Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Merchant Platform - Tessellate Finance

Tessellate Finance offers business banking and card issuing for small
merchants. Our product engineers own features end to end, from the React
frontend through the infrastructure they run on.

What you'll do
- Build merchant-facing features in React, TypeScript, and Next.js backed by
  our GraphQL API.
- Own the infrastructure for your services: every environment is defined in
  Terraform, and you'll write and review Terraform modules weekly.
- Write tests with Jest and Playwright.

Requirements
- 5+ years building production web applications with React and TypeScript.
- Production experience with Terraform (required; we will not consider
  candidates without hands-on Terraform experience).
- Experience in fintech, payments, or banking.
