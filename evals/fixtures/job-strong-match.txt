Senior Frontend Engineer, Payments - Corvid Pay

Corvid Pay builds checkout and payout infrastructure for online merchants.
We're hiring a Senior Frontend Engineer to own the merchant dashboard and
hosted checkout experience.

What you'll do
- Build and ship features across our hosted checkout and merchant dashboard
  using React, TypeScript, and Next.js.
- Design GraphQL queries and client-side caching for data-heavy views.
- Raise the quality bar: unit tests with Jest, end-to-end tests with
  Playwright, and accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA).
- Work with compliance on PCI-DSS requirements for card-entry flows.
- Mentor other engineers and lead small feature squads.

What you bring
- 5+ years of professional frontend experience with React and TypeScript.
- Experience in payments, banking, or other regulated fintech products.
- A track record of leading features end to end with a small team.

Nice to have
- Experience with design systems and Storybook.
