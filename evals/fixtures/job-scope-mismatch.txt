Director of Engineering, Frontend Platform - Corvid Pay

Corvid Pay builds checkout and payout infrastructure for online merchants.
We're hiring a Director of Engineering to lead our frontend organization.

What you'll do
- Lead the frontend organization: 4 engineering managers and roughly 40
  engineers across the merchant dashboard, hosted checkout, and design system
  teams.
- Own the organization's headcount plan, hiring, and a multi-million-dollar
  annual budget.
- Set the multi-year technical strategy for our React, TypeScript, and
  Next.js platform, including our GraphQL client architecture.
- Represent frontend engineering at the executive staff level and partner with
  VPs of Product and Design on roadmap planning.
- Grow managers: coach, run calibration, and build career ladders.

What you bring
- 12+ years in software engineering, including 5+ years managing managers.
- Deep hands-on background in React and TypeScript.
- Experience in payments or other regulated fintech products.
- A record of scaling an engineering organization through rapid growth.
