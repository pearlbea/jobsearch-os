Senior Embedded Firmware Engineer - Halden Automotive

Halden Automotive designs electronic control units (ECUs) for electric
vehicle battery management systems.

Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain safety-critical firmware in C and C++ for ARM
  Cortex-M microcontrollers running FreeRTOS.
- Implement CAN bus and LIN communication stacks.
- Bring up new boards with hardware engineers using oscilloscopes, logic
  analyzers, and JTAG debuggers.
- Produce ISO 26262 (ASIL-C) safety documentation and MISRA C compliant code.

Requirements
- 6+ years of embedded C development on bare-metal or RTOS targets.
- Hands-on experience with automotive communication protocols (CAN, LIN).
- Experience working to ISO 26262 or similar functional safety standards.
- Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or similar.

Halden Automotive is an equal opportunity employer.
